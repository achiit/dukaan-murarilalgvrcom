About the shop

A family name on a Govardhan signboard

We started this shop in 2017 on Deeg Road with one simple idea: whoever walks in, a household or a small shopkeeper, should get fresh stock at an honest rate.

The shop carries my family's name, and I treat every packet that leaves it as a promise. Customers who came in the first year still come today, and many of them now send their children to pick up the order.

If you run a shop and need regular supply, or you just need bread and milk for the morning, you are welcome here.

  • Wholesale and retail from the same counter
  • Amul, Hovis and Bonn, all brands you already trust
  • Open early at 7 AM, closes at 9 PM
  • Order by phone or WhatsApp, no need to visit first
  • Located on Deeg Road, easy to find near Shri Dham Colony
